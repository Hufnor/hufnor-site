Before you order

Questions people ask us.

Short answers. Every one of them is also on the product page it applies to.

Why PETG instead of PLA?

PLA softens around 140 °F and a parked car beats that easily. Catalog parts are printed in PETG unless the product page says otherwise, so they hold their shape in a hot cabin, a garage in July or a sunny windowsill.

How long until it ships?

Each part is printed for your order in Overland Park, Kansas. Parts in black or white ship within 5 business days; other colors add up to 7 business days while we order the filament. Custom parts ship once you approve the final design. We email tracking when it ships. US addresses only, $5.99 flat per order, shown at checkout.

Will it fit my car, board or box?

Fitment is listed on every product page: the Civic parts fit 2016–2021 sedan, coupe, hatchback, Si and Type R (not the 2022+ 11th generation); the Skadis mounts fit every IKEA Skadis board size; the Klein inserts fit the 54500MBX half-width component box. Not sure? Send a photo and a rough size through the contact form and we’ll tell you before you buy.

What if it doesn’t fit or doesn’t work for me?

Tell us within 30 days of delivery. Send it back unused and we refund the part price (shipping isn’t refunded), or we revise the design until it fits. Custom and commissioned parts aren’t returnable, but we keep revising until they’re right. Details on the shipping & returns page.

What does the warranty cover?

One year from the day it ships. Parts under $100 are replaced free (you cover postage); parts over $100 are replaced at the cost of material plus shipping. We ask for a photo of the break or the part itself. Full terms are in the warranty section.

Can I get a different size, layout or color?

Yes. Catalog parts ship fastest in black or white PETG; 11 other PETG colors are made to order and add up to 7 business days. Most can be reprinted at another size. A one-off drawn to your measurements has no minimum and no design fee; you get a quote inside a week, and most land between $10 and $500.

What counts as a commission?

Bigger builds: equipment, machines and mechanisms engineered end to end. Those start with a free call so we can hear what it has to do before anyone talks numbers. Use the contact form and pick “A commission.”

Where’s my order?

Use the order status page with the code from your thank-you page or receipt (it starts with BR-). It shows Queued → Printing → Packed → Shipped, and the tracking number once it leaves.

Who actually makes this?

Two mechanical engineers, Michael Huffman and Zach Minor, in the Kansas City area. We design the parts, print them on our own machines and answer the email ourselves. Read more about us.

Custom Gridfinity-compatible bins

Send three photos.
Get a bin cut to your tool.

Lay the tool on a sheet of printer paper, take three phone photos, and upload them here. We turn the photos into a true-size outline, cut a pocket for it in a Gridfinity-compatible bin or a plain tray, and send you a quote before anything is printed.

Upload photosHow it works

  1. You take three photos

    The tool lying flat on plain Letter or A4 paper, the same tool on its side, and its brand and model label. No calipers, no printer, no mailing anything.

  2. We trace and check it

    Software finds the paper's corners, flattens the photo to true scale and cuts out the tool's outline. The side photo sets the pocket depth. A person checks every outline before it goes further.

  3. Quote, then print

    We email a preview and a price within two business days. Once you approve, we cut the pocket with clearance and a finger notch, print it and ship it from Overland Park.

The three photos

1 · From above

Tool flat on the paper, phone straight overhead.

  • All four paper corners in the frame
  • Stand up and use 2× zoom (about 4 ft away)
  • No flash; avoid hard shadows from a window

2 · On its side

Same tool, same paper, turned on its edge.

  • Shows how thick it is, which sets pocket depth
  • Same height and zoom as photo 1

3 · The label

Brand and model, close up.

  • Lets us check the outline against published dimensions
  • Skip it if the tool has no label

What to expect. Phone photos measure to within about a millimetre, so we design every pocket with a little clearance and a chamfered edge. Tools drop in and sit secure; it is a snug custom fit, not a skin-tight mould. White or silver tools on white paper are hard to see. Use a sheet of colored paper for those. Very rounded or tall tools may need an extra photo; we'll ask.
